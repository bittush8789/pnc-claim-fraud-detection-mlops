Business Problem & System Capabilities

Property & Casualty (P&C) insurance fraud costs insurers billions of dollars annually. Common indicators include stage collisions, lack of police documentation, short policy lifetime prior to incident, and mismatch between coverage amounts and claimed damages.

ShieldGuard utilizes automated feature engineering and ensemble ML models (Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forests, XGBoost, and LightGBM) to deliver high-recall risk scores and explanations, preventing leakage and prioritizing investigators' workload.
